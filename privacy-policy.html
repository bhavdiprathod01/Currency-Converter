Privacy Policy

Currency Converter app  |  Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: Currency Converter doesn’t ask you to create an account and doesn’t collect your name, email, or phone number. The app uses the internet to fetch exchange rates, and it shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some device information as described below.

1. Who we are

Currency Converter (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile application for Android that converts amounts between currencies. The app is operated by [Your Name or Company Name]. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [your-email@example.com].

2. Information we collect

We do not require registration and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app does keep a few simple settings on your device only, such as the currencies you last selected. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Exchange rate data

To show current exchange rates, the app sends requests over the internet to the Frankfurter API. These requests contain the currency codes needed to look up rates (for example, USD and INR). Like any web service, the API server receives your device’s IP address when the request is made. We do not control the API’s server logs; please refer to the Frankfurter website for how it handles data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • App interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks

This information is collected by Google, not by us. How Google uses it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path can vary by device).
  • Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

5. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs:

  • Internet access – to fetch exchange rates and to load ads.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

6. How we use information

The limited data described above is used only to provide the currency conversion service and to display ads that help keep the app available. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Sharing of information

We do not share personal information with third parties, except for the service providers mentioned in this policy (Frankfurter API and Google AdMob), which process data as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law.

8. Data retention and security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data held by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. Settings saved on your device remain until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. We take reasonable steps to protect the app, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, or withdrawing consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests concern data held by Google; you can manage that through your Google Account privacy controls. You can also contact us at the email above with any request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please review it periodically.
